About

Hello! I'm Jeremy, a Junior at the University of Notre Dame studying Computer Science. I'm also on the Men's Swim Team. I grew up in Georgetown, Texas, and transferred to Notre Dame from the University of Texas at Austin after my freshman year.


At Notre Dame, I've really enjoyed exploring the applications of computer science to real-world problems, and I've been fortunate to have the opportunity to work on some really interesting projects. You can read more about my projects here, and you can also check out my GitHub for more of my work.


I'm very passionate about all things AI/ML, and I enjoy exploring applications of these technologies to real-world problems. Teaching is also a passion of mine - I love learning and want to help other people do the same. I believe that the best way to learn is by doing, and I'm always looking for new opportunities to apply what I've learned.


This past summer, I studied abroad in Dublin, Ireland, where I took courses on Machine Learning and the engineering design process. Once I returned, I developed a dashboard for NCAA swimming results that allows users to visualize historical swimming data in a more interactive way. I also competed at US Nationals in Irvine, California, where I set a team record in the 200m IM.

In terms of work experience, I most recently completed a microinternship with Eli Lilly and Company, where I worked on a team to design a digital solution for early detection of Alzheimer's disease. Additionally, I am a cofounder of the nonprofit organization Ten at a Time, which I remained involved with for over ten years. I also worked as a cave tour guide.


Outside of school, I spend most of my time with friends and family, exploring nature, hiking, and traveling whenever I get the chance. I'm a huge fan of all kinds of movies, music, anime, and sci-fi / fantasy books. I also love good food, and I'd like to own a bakery someday.
